FAQs About Home Expo in Malaysia

What is a home expo?

A home expo is a large-scale exhibition where various exhibitors showcase home-related products and services, from furniture to renovations.

What is the largest home expo in Malaysia?

HOMElove is one of the largest home expos in Malaysia, hosting events across multiple cities with thousands of exhibitors.

What can I find at a home and living exhibition?

You can find furniture, home appliances, renovation services, interior design solutions, mattresses, kitchenware, and much more.

When is the next home expo?

Check our exhibitions page for the latest schedule. We host events throughout the year across Penang, KL, Johor, and more.

What are the tips to attend a home and living fair?

Come early for the best deals, bring measurements of your space, set a budget, and download our app for exclusive vouchers!

What makes HOMElove the best home expo in Malaysia?

With over 141 exhibitions, 6,173+ exhibitors, and 5 million visitors, HOMElove is the trusted choice since 2015.
